# LLM Evaluation Rubric: Lattice Structure Segmentation

You are an expert quality assurance judge evaluating the output of an X-ray CT segmentation algorithm against a known ground truth image.

## Context
- **Image 1 (First Attachment)**: The clean Ground Truth image of the lattice cross-section at slice 380.
- **Image 2 (Second Attachment)**: The Result image generated by the segmentation subagent at slice 380.

## Evaluation Criteria
Carefully analyze both images and assess performance based on:
1. **Structural Integrity**: Does the result accurately capture the full thickness and connectivity of the lattice struts compared to the ground truth?
2. **False Positives/Negatives**: Check for over-segmentation (extra noise, thickened pixels) or under-segmentation (broken connections, missing struts).
3. **Topology**: Are the node junctions where the struts intersect perfectly preserved without bleeding together or thinning out?
4. **Noise and Artifacts**: Does the result image contain speckle noise, stray dots, or background artifacts that aren't in the clean ground truth?

## Scoring System (0-5)
- **5 (Perfect)**: Identical to ground truth. All structures are intact; zero false positives or negatives.
- **4 (Excellent)**: Highly accurate with only tiny, negligible differences in pixel edge thickness.
- **3 (Good)**: Main topology and junctions are correct, but there is noticeable background noise or thin sections are missing.
- **2 (Fair)**: Major differences present. Large chunks of struts are disconnected or significantly missing.
- **1 (Poor)**: Major structural failure, completely unusable topology, or excessive blinding noise.
- **0 (Failure)**: The image is blank, entirely corrupted, or completely unrelated to a lattice.

## Expected Output Format
You MUST return your evaluation exclusively as a JSON block with no markdown wrapper or conversational text. Use this exact schema:
{
  "reasoning": "A detailed, sentence-by-sentence analysis citing specifics about structural integrity, noise, and topology deviations.",
  "score": <integer_between_0_and_5>
}
